For students

Everything you check, and everything you study, together.

Attendance, marks and results

Your attendance in every subject, the marks of each test, your SGPA and CGPA, worked out exactly and flagged before they become a problem.

Timetable and notices

Today's classes at a glance, the full week, and every notice and deadline from your college in one list.

Study material

Notes, past papers, lab manuals and model papers for your subjects. Search them, preview them in the page and bookmark the ones you need.

Quick Exam

Timed practice tests on one unit. The answers are marked on the server and explained as soon as you finish.

Progress

See which units are strong and which need work, judged on your own recent answers, with notes to revise from.

AI study assistant

Explanations, summaries and practice questions for your subjects. Clearly labelled, and it never sees your records.

For colleges

The records stay with the college.

Administrators keep every record in one place. Students only read their own; what they may change is their phone number, a few lines about themselves and their photo.

  • Student accounts Create, find, deactivate and reset accounts.
  • Attendance and marks Fill a whole class sheet at once.
  • Results Record semester results; CGPA follows.
  • Timetable and notices Plan the week and publish news.
  • Resources Upload PDFs that are checked first.
  • Audit log Changes to marks, attendance, results and accounts are kept.

Privacy

Built to keep your records yours.

  • Passwords stay secret

    Stored only in a protected form that nobody can read back, not even an administrator.

  • Your records are yours

    The server checks every request. Another student can never open your marks, attendance or tests.

  • Files stay private

    Study material sits in private storage and opens through links that stop working after five minutes.

  • AI without your data

    The assistant sees only what you type for one request. Your name and records are never sent to it.

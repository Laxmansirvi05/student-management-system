Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

About this version. Campus Companion is a student project. The demonstration runs on invented students only. Before it is used with the records of real students, the college must review this page, adopt it, and name the person who is responsible for it.

1. What this page covers

Campus Companion shows students their college records and helps them study. This page says what the application keeps about you, why, who can see it, and what you can do about it. It applies to students and to administrators.

2. What is kept about you

  • Your account. Your institutional login email, your name, whether you are a student or an administrator, and when you last logged in. Your password is kept only in a protected form (a BCrypt hash) that cannot be turned back into the password. Nobody, including an administrator, can read it.
  • Your college record, entered by the college: roll number, branch, semester, section and admission year, and your attendance, marks and semester results.
  • What you add yourself, all optional: a phone number, a short text about you, and a profile photo.
  • What you do here. The study material you bookmark, and your quick tests: the questions you were given, your answers and your scores.
  • Your use of the AI assistant, as numbers only: how many requests you made, which tool you used, how long the texts were, and whether the request worked.
  • A security record. When marks, attendance, results, an account or a password are changed, the application records who changed what and when, and the network address the change came from. A password itself is never written into this record.

3. What is not kept

  • The text you send to the AI assistant and the answer it gives are not stored. If you want to keep an answer, copy it.
  • A phone writes the place and time into the photos it takes. That information is removed before your profile photo is stored: the application redraws the picture and keeps only the new one.
  • There is no advertising, no tracking of what you do on other sites, and no analytics service.

4. Cookies and what is stored in your browser

The application uses two cookies, and both are needed for it to work. One keeps you logged in; it ends when you log out, or after 30 minutes without use. The other is a security token that protects the forms you send. Neither is used to follow you.

If you switch on "Reduce motion" in Settings, that choice is remembered in your browser. While you take a quick test, the test is remembered in the open tab so that reloading the page does not lose it.

5. Who can see what

  • You see your own record, and nobody else's. Another student cannot see your attendance, marks, results, tests, bookmarks, phone number or photo.
  • Administrators of your college can see and correct student records, create and deactivate accounts, and reset a password. They cannot see a password.
  • Nobody outside your college is given access.

6. Where it is kept and who helps to run the service

  • The database and the files (study material and profile photos) are kept with Supabase. The files are private: one can be opened only through a link that the application gives to a logged-in person and that stops working after five minutes.
  • The application itself runs on a hosting service chosen by the college.
  • When you use the AI assistant, the text you type or paste for that request is sent to an AI service so that it can write the answer. Your name, roll number, email address and records are never sent to it. That is why the assistant asks you not to paste personal information. While the assistant is in demo mode, no AI service is used at all.

7. How long it is kept

  • Your account and your college record are kept for as long as the college needs them for your studies.
  • The security record is kept for as long as the service runs, because its purpose is to show later what was changed.
  • The numbers about your use of the AI assistant are meant to be removed after 90 days. Removing them automatically is not built yet, so for now they are kept until an administrator removes them.
  • Your phone number, the text about you and your photo are kept until you remove them or your account is closed.

8. How it is protected

  • Every page and every request needs you to be logged in, and the application checks on the server that a record is yours before it shows it.
  • After five wrong passwords in a row, logging in to that account is blocked for 15 minutes.
  • A new student account starts with a temporary password that must be changed at the first login.
  • Files are checked before they are stored, and are never served from the application's own address.

No service can promise that nothing will ever go wrong. If you think someone else has used your account, change your password at once and tell your college administrator.

9. What you can do

  • Change your password at any time, in Settings.
  • Change or remove your phone number, the text about you and your photo, on your profile.
  • Ask your college administrator to correct a record that is wrong.
  • Ask your college what it keeps about you, and ask for your account to be closed when you no longer need it.

10. Questions and changes

For any question about this page or about your records, speak to your college administrator. When this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Terms of use

Last updated: 14 October 2026

About this version. Campus Companion is a student project. The demonstration runs on invented students only. Before it is used with the records of real students, the college must review this page, adopt it, and name the person who is responsible for it.

1. What Campus Companion is

Campus Companion lets a student see their attendance, marks, results, timetable and notices, read study material, take practice tests and use an AI study assistant. It lets the college's administrators keep those records. By logging in, you agree to these terms.

2. Your account

  • Your account is for you alone. Do not share your password and do not use another person's account.
  • A new account starts with a temporary password. Change it at your first login.
  • If you think someone else knows your password, change it and tell your college administrator.
  • There is no "forgot password" form. An administrator resets a password after checking that the request is yours.

3. What you must not do

  • Do not try to see or change another person's records.
  • Do not try to get around the limits or the protections of the application, or to overload it.
  • Do not upload anything that is not yours to use, or that is harmful, offensive or unlawful. A profile photo should be a suitable picture of yourself.
  • Do not pass on study material beyond your own use where the college has not allowed it.

4. Your records

The attendance, marks and results shown here are entered by your college. If what you see here differs from the college's official record, the official record is the one that counts. If you find a mistake, ask your college administrator to correct it.

5. Study material

The notes, papers and manuals are provided by your college for your study. A link to a file is made for you and works for a few minutes only. Past question papers and model papers show what was asked before; they do not say what will be asked next.

6. Quick tests and progress

Quick tests are for practice. They do not count towards your marks, and your college does not use them to judge you. The test is timed and marked by the application. The advice on what to revise comes from fixed rules applied to your own answers.

7. The AI study assistant

  • Its answers are written by a computer program and can be wrong or incomplete. Check them against your course material. They are not an official statement of your college.
  • It does not know your marks, attendance, results, notices, syllabus or exam dates, and it cannot tell you what will be in an exam.
  • Do not paste personal information about yourself or anyone else. The text you submit is sent to an AI service.
  • There is a limit on how many requests you can make in a day.
  • It does not mark tests and it does not decide anything about you.

8. Availability

The service is provided as it is. It may be slow or unavailable at times, and parts of it, such as file storage or the AI assistant, can be unavailable while the rest works. Do not rely on it as your only copy of anything you need.

9. Closing an account

Your college can deactivate an account, for example when a student leaves or when these terms are broken. A deactivated account cannot log in.

10. Changes and questions

These terms can change; the date at the top shows the current version. For any question, speak to your college administrator. How your information is handled is described in the privacy policy.
